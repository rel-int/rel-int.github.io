# Tiny Recursive Message Passing

Message passing neural networks encode the structure of a task directly in their architecture. This paper trains them with the procedures of recursive transformers: deep supervision, truncated recursions and test-time deduction. The two are complementary: they lead to small models that transfer zero-shot to larger instances than those seen in training.

By [Tommaso Salvatori](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=fG9vZC4AAAAJ), [Matteo Forasassi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=md96RmMAAAAJ), [Alexis Toumi](http://alexis.toumi.xyz/) and [Giovanni de Felice](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=-_bkN1gAAAAJ&hl=en).
