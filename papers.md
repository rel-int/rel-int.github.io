# Papers

## Recent work

- **[Tiny Recursive Message Passing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18649)** [Tommaso Salvatori](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=fG9vZC4AAAAJ), [Matteo Forasassi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=md96RmMAAAAJ), [Alexis Toumi](https://alexis.toumi.xyz/) and [Giovanni de Felice](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=-_bkN1gAAAAJ&hl=en), 2026. Message passing networks trained with the procedures of recursive transformers give small models for constraint reasoning.
- **[Circular Predictive Coding Unlocks Effective Depth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27610)** [Tommaso Salvatori](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=fG9vZC4AAAAJ) and [Matteo Forasassi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=md96RmMAAAAJ), 2026. Circular predictive coding networks get their depth from energy minimization, rather than from stacking layers.
- **[A Quantum Machine Learning Approach to Content-Based Media Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21562)** Mina Abbaszadeh et al., 2026. Text descriptions are mapped to quantum circuits using their semantic structure, to recommend media by content.

## Earlier work

- [Scalable and interpretable quantum natural language processing: an implementation on trapped ions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.08777), Tiffany Duneau et al., 2024: the first implementation of text-level quantum natural language processing.
- [Quantum Algorithms for Compositional Text Processing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06061), Tuomas Laakkonen et al., 2024: QDisCoCirc, a quantum adaptation of DisCoCirc, with quantum algorithms for question answering.
- [Experimental quantum-enhanced kernels on a photonic processor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.20364), Zhenghao Yin et al., 2024: a kernel method for binary classification on a photonic integrated processor.
- [Towards Compositional Interpretability for XAI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17583), Sean Tull et al., 2024: AI models and their interpretability defined with category theory.
- [The Mathematics of Text Structure](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.03478), Bob Coecke, 2019: DisCoCirc, how sentences interact to give the meaning of a text.
- [Generalized relations in linguistics and cognition](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2018.03.008), Bob Coecke et al., 2018: categories of generalized relations as models for cognition and natural language processing.
- [From quantum foundations via natural language meaning to a theory of everything](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.07618), Bob Coecke, 2016: how interaction between quantum systems carries over to how word meanings interact.
